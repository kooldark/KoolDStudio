## ✨ Design Philosophy Applied

- **Minimalist Copy** - Every sentence serves a purpose
- **Emotional Language** - "yêu thương" (loved), "con tim" (heart), "trái tim" (heart)
- **Art-Centric** - "tác phẩm nghệ thuật" (artwork), "Art of Your Story"
- **Active Voice** - Direct, present tense narration
- **Poetic Touches** - "mãi mãi" (forever), sensory details (ánh mắt, nụ cười)
- **Consistency** - Unified voice across all sections
